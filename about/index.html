A lot of my life seems to have orbited around the public library. My mom's a librarian, and I grew up spending a lot of time tucked between shelves, developing a stout love of the Library as a larger entity.

Nowadays, it's become habit to visit new local libraries wherever I go. I wanted a space to highlight these stories and thoughts, hence this card-catalog-inspired site. Flip through, stay awhile, then pay a visit to your local library!
